Marquee Rush

Numbers from 1 to 100 light up around the ring, one at a time. For every number, click the emoji that matches the rules. Most correct answers after the final round wins.

Open games

    Connecting to the room...

    • The host picks how many numbers each round shows (10 to 99). They are random numbers from 1 to 100, in ascending order around the ring. Up to 2 games can run at once, with up to 40 players each.
    • Three to six emojis sit in the middle. Each number lights for the time the host sets (3 seconds by default), and everyone clicks the emoji the rules give for that number.
    • Before every round, random players each draft one rule from three choices (one fewer than the number of emojis, so 2 players with 3 emojis, 5 with 6). A rule links numbers to an emoji, for example odd numbers: the pizza. Every other number uses the round's default emoji.
    • After every round the leaderboard stays up until the host starts the next round. You can join a game at any time, even mid-round. Keep this tab open while you play. It works on phones too.
