Connecting to the room...

  • The ring has one bulb per player, with a minimum of 10. Three emojis appear in the middle each round. Bulbs light one at a time, and when yours lights you click the right emoji.
  • Your number stays hidden until 5 bulbs before it lights.
  • Before every round, two random players each draft one rule from three choices. A rule links numbers to an emoji, for example odd numbers: the pizza. Every other number uses the round's default emoji.
  • Keep this tab open while you play. It works on phones too.
